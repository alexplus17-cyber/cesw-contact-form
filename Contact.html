Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our CMMI assessment consultation and software quality services.

How do I schedule a CMMI assessment consultation?

To schedule a CMMI assessment consultation, you can contact CESW via our online form, email info@cesw.org, or call +1-555-123-4567. We typically respond within 24 hours and offer free initial consultations to understand your software quality goals and recommend the most appropriate assessment approach for your organization. During the consultation, we'll discuss your current processes, maturity goals, timeline, and budget to create a customized assessment plan.

What information should I prepare before contacting CESW for a SCAMPI assessment?

Before contacting CESW for a SCAMPI assessment, prepare information about your organization's size, current development processes, previous CMMI maturity level (if any), specific goals for the assessment, timeline requirements, and any process documentation you have available. This helps us provide an accurate assessment scope and cost estimate during your initial consultation. Having this information readily available accelerates the planning process and ensures we understand your unique context and requirements.

Does CESW offer remote CMMI assessment services?

Yes, CESW provides comprehensive remote CMMI assessment services including SCAMPI appraisals, process improvement consulting, and training workshops. Our virtual consultation capabilities allow us to serve organizations worldwide with the same quality and thoroughness as on-site engagements, using secure collaboration tools and video conferencing platforms. Remote assessments have become increasingly popular and can be equally effective when conducted by experienced assessors using appropriate methodologies and tools.

What is the cost of a CMMI assessment consultation?

Initial CMMI assessment consultations with CESW are complimentary. During this consultation, we evaluate your organization's needs and provide a detailed proposal with transparent pricing for your specific assessment or consulting engagement. Assessment costs vary based on organization size, scope, maturity level targeted, and whether services are delivered on-site or remotely. We pride ourselves on providing clear, upfront pricing with no hidden fees, allowing you to budget effectively for your process improvement journey.

How quickly can CESW begin a software process improvement engagement?

CESW typically begins software process improvement engagements within 2-4 weeks of contract signing. For urgent needs, we offer accelerated scheduling options. The duration of engagements varies based on scope - gap analyses may take 2-3 weeks, while comprehensive process improvement programs typically span 6-18 months depending on your target maturity level and organizational readiness. We work with you to establish realistic timelines that account for your operational constraints while maintaining momentum toward your improvement objectives.
